## Objective
Analyze business performance using the Superstore Sales Dataset.

## Tools Used
* Python
* Pandas
* Matplotlib
* Google Colab

## Analysis Performed
### Monthly Revenue Trends

* Analyzed monthly sales revenue.
* Visualized revenue trends using a line chart.

### Top-Selling Products

* Identified the top 10 best-selling products.
* Visualized product sales using a bar chart.

### Profit Analysis

* Analyzed profit performance by category and product.
* Visualized profit distribution.

## Key Insights

* Sales varied across different months.
* Some products generated significantly higher sales than others.
* Technology products contributed strongly to overall business performance.
* Profit analysis helped identify high-performing and low-performing products.

## Conclusion

The analysis provided insights into sales trends, product performance, and profitability. These findings can help improve business decisions and sales strategies.
